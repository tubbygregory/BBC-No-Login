Add Never to optional sign-in reminders. Follow these steps to install BBC No Login in your desktop browser.

1

KEEP IT SOMEWHERE SAFE

Choose a home for this folder

Move the extracted folder somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder. Keep all the files together, including this guide and manifest.json.

Your browser will load the extension from this folder, so keep it after installation.

2

OPEN YOUR EXTENSIONS

Choose your browser

Your browser

Copy this address and paste it into your browser’s address bar.

3

ADD THE EXTENSION

Enable Developer mode

Developer mode is in the top-right corner.

Turn it on, choose Load unpacked, then select the folder containing this guide and manifest.json.

Select the folder, not the ZIP.BBC No Login should now appear in your extensions list. Make sure it is enabled.
4

MAKE IT YOURS

Reload your BBC tabs

When a supported sign-in reminder appears, choose Never. Your choice is saved across BBC websites in this browser profile.

To enable it straight away, open BBC No Login from your browser’s extensions menu and turn on Never ask me to sign in.

You can change your mind at any time from the same menu. Pin the extension to your toolbar for easier access.

A little clarity

This extension handles optional, English-language sign-in reminders on BBC websites. Services that require an account, including iPlayer, still require one.

It needs access to BBC pages to add Never, and local storage to remember your choice.

Read the privacy policy

Work or school browser? Your administrator may control whether you can load extensions this way.

Change your mind

Open BBC No Login and choose Allow reminders again. Future reminders can appear; the one you dismissed stays closed.

Keep it up to date

GitHub installs do not update automatically. Replace the files in the same folder, then choose Reload on your extensions page.
